## 🎨 Quick Customizations

### Change Buy Link
**File:** `/app/frontend/src/App.js`  
**Find:** `href="https://www.aliexpress.us/item/..."`  
**Replace with:** Your Shopify product URL

### Update Price
**File:** `/app/frontend/src/App.js`  
**Find:** `$14.99`  
**Replace with:** Your price

### Change Images
**File:** `/app/frontend/src/App.js`  
**Look for:** `productImages` object  
**Update:** URLs with your product images

### After Changes:
```bash
cd /app/frontend
yarn build
```
